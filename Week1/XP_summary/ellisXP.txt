Here is a summary of what each XP role represents in my understanding:
Tracker: takes care of how people with different roles are going with their tasks, if something is going off then the tracker is responsible to move things back on track

Customer: responsible for user stories while representing users and stakeholders, often also defines the priorities within the sprint

Programmer: elaborates the stories into programs with unit tests to fit in a certain time frame

Coach: guides the team, and ensuring the XP practices is followed

Manager: handles communications with the client

Tester: design and run tests to ensure the program if free of bugs

Doomsayer: predicts and identifies potential risks that could happen
